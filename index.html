    The evergreen priority orders
    Seed Shop — buy order

    The Seed Shop is a major T3 lever. Community-recommended purchase order:

    1. Auto-seed system — automate seeding first; everything downstream leans on it.
    2. Item Rarity (HS) — big multiplier on gear quality.
    3. Subclass EXP & Portal HP–ATK reduction — speeds portal pushing.
    4. Bonus Seeds — worth it once it's ~0.5× your manual seeds.
    5. Portal HP reduction → Global Bonus → Subclass bonuses → SWP / SKP upgrades.
    Classes & talent order

    All 7 classes — Farmer, Hoer, Smasher, Digger, Rancher, Harvester, Freeloader. Levels in every class grant talent points for your current class, so level them in parallel. You gain a talent point per 10 levels, plus a permanent +1 the first time each class crosses a multiple of 100 (re-granted every Reincarnation).

    • Talent tree order: Freeloader first (raises equipment levels), then Smasher > Hoer > Farmer > Freeloader.
    • Keystone rotation: Harvester (worm find), Rancher (+milk), Freeloader (protein / equipment).
    • A55+ role split: Digger → Seed Shop / mining · Farmer → gem farming · Rancher → world stages · Freeloader → Protein.

    Farming — quick rules
    • Auto-plot first. It takes precedence over every other French Fry Corner upgrade.
    • Extra starting plot only once it costs under 10% of a run's fry.
    • PIC upgrades right before a farm reset, and after the first harvests of a newly-unlocked plant.
    • Reset at ~10× fry earned, or when meaningful page-4 upgrades are in reach. Avoid runs over ~5 days until late game.
    • Contagion: funnel grasshoppers into FRY near reset; otherwise spread evenly.
    • AFK = plant one of each crop; active = plant only your highest tier, then rotate down.
